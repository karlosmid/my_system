# Lesson 02 - Mobile Center

Concepts
- A mobile center can advance to gain space or restrict the opponent.
- Pawn breaks decide whether the center remains flexible or becomes fixed.
- Central mobility should be prepared, not rushed.

- Correctly saw that White's mobile center gives White space.
- Correctly preferred developing pieces before rushing central pawns forward.
- Needs to sharpen the meaning of a pawn break: a pawn move that directly attacks the opponent's pawn structure and tries to open, exchange, or fix the center.
- In this structure, Black's main central breaks are `...e5` against `♙d4` and `...c5` against `♙d4`.
- Advancing `d5` gains space but gives up the tension against `e5`; advancing `e5` attacks `♞f6` and gains space, but may leave central squares and pawn breaks for Black.
